SOLMAR: NEON HEIST

You are Dario Vega, a wheelman with one last night in Solmar — a neon city that never sleeps. Run three jobs: grab the package, lose the heat, crack the bank safe — then escape to the yacht with the money.

WASD / ARROWS — walk & drive  •  E — steal / exit car, hold E at the bank to crack the safe
MOUSE CLICK — shoot (on foot)  •  R — restart after busted / wasted

Crimes raise your wanted stars. Hide from the cops until the heat cools off. Get caught on foot and you're BUSTED. Lose all health and you're WASTED.

SOLMAR: Neon Heist — a make.fun entry. Open index.html in any modern browser.
Tip: steal a fast car first. The pink marker is your first job.
